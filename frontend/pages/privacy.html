Privacy

Your data,
plainly

This page describes how CampusSphere handles data. It is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

  • Account data: your name, email and a salted password hash are stored in the project's database. Passwords are never stored in plain text.
  • Session: signing in sets an HTTP-only cookie that identifies your session. Signing out ends the session on the server.
  • Records you create: event and course registrations, saved destinations and appointment requests are linked to your account and visible only to you (employee records are visible to administrators only).
  • Checkout: no card details are collected and nothing is stored.
  • AI Assistant: the messages you send are passed through the server to an AI service to generate a reply. Do not share sensitive personal information in the chat.
  • Voice: dictation and read-aloud use your browser's built-in features. CampusSphere does not record or store audio, but your browser may process dictation audio with its own speech service.
  • Labs: keystrokes and mouse movements in the labs stay in your browser.
