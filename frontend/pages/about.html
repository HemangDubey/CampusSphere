About CampusSphere

A smarter way
to campus

CampusSphere is a student project built for a Front End Design and Testing course. It brings a student dashboard, campus events, a travel explorer, campus resources, a demonstration healthcare section, a demonstration checkout, interactive event labs and an AI Assistant into one app.

Much of the content is sample or demonstration data and is labelled that way where you see it. CampusSphere is not connected to any university system and does not show official campus information.
