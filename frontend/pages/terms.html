CampusSphere is a student coursework project provided as is, for demonstration only.

  • Listings, prices, doctors, hospitals and events are sample data and must not be relied on.
  • Healthcare features are not a medical service and no real appointment is booked. In an emergency, contact local emergency services.
  • The checkout is a demonstration: no real payment is made and no transaction is created.
  • The AI Assistant can be wrong and has no access to official campus data. Verify important information with your institution.
  • Please do not enter real sensitive personal data into this demonstration.
